# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, harassment-free community, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity or orientation.

## Expected conduct

Treat others with empathy and respect; welcome different perspectives; give and accept constructive feedback; take responsibility for mistakes; and consider the community's well-being. Harassment, sexualized language or unwelcome attention, insults, personal attacks, publishing private information without consent, and other inappropriate professional conduct are not acceptable.

## Scope and responsibility

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. The maintainer may remove or reject contributions and take fair corrective action, explaining decisions where appropriate.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature on the repository Security tab. Reports will be reviewed fairly and as promptly as possible, with respect for the reporter's privacy and security.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer considers the community impact and circumstances when deciding how to respond:

1. **Correction:** For unwelcome or unprofessional behavior, the maintainer may privately explain the problem and issue a written warning. An apology may also be requested.
2. **Warning:** For a violation or repeated behavior, the maintainer may set a period during which the person must not interact with the people involved, including those enforcing this code, in project spaces or elsewhere. Further violations can lead to a ban.
3. **Temporary ban:** For serious or sustained violations, the maintainer may prohibit participation and public communication with the community for a specified time. Contact with the people involved or with those enforcing this code is not permitted during the ban; further violations can result in a permanent ban.
4. **Permanent ban:** A pattern of serious misconduct, harassment, aggression, or disparagement of groups may result in a permanent prohibition on community participation and public interaction.
